Course topics and lessons

Work through 28 topics across four weeks, starting with the foundations of prompting and ending with dependable AI features. Each week combines lessons, practice, and graded work.

  • Week 1: Foundations

    How models respond, prompt anatomy, specificity, constraints, and common failure modes.

  • Week 2: Core techniques

    Few-shot examples, task decomposition, context, delimiters, and prompt debugging.

  • Week 3: Advanced patterns

    Structured output, chaining, retrieval, tool use, sampling, and evaluation.

  • Week 4: Applications and safety

    Real work, prompt injection, hallucination, bias, reusable templates, and AI systems.
