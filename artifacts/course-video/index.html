Walkthrough details

  1. The dashboard gives learners a clear view of assignments, practice problems, their current module, and recent activity.
  2. The curriculum covers four weeks and twenty-eight topics, from foundations and core techniques to advanced patterns, applications, and safety.
  3. Lessons are available in short, medium, and long versions. For example, a few-shot prompting lesson explains how examples guide a model toward the pattern you need.
  4. When learners have a question, the section-scoped tutor uses the passage they are reading. It can explain why a model adds conversational filler even when JSON was requested.
  5. Adaptive practice asks learners to explain their reasoning, gives feedback, and adjusts difficulty as they build a streak.
  6. Assignments are graded with written rationale. Learners can see their score, understand the concepts they demonstrated, and review an authorship check.
  7. Performance analytics show course progress, practice accuracy, attempts, streaks, and the topics that need more work.
  8. Prompt Engineering brings the curriculum, the tutor, the grader, and the integrity check together in one room.
